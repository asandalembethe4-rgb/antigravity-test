About The Studio

AG Photography — Capturing What Truly Matters

At AG Photography, photography is far more than pressing a shutter — it is the delicate art of honoring milestones, preserving authentic emotions, and transforming transient moments into timeless heirlooms.

Photography Booking Terms & Conditions

1. Booking Deposit

A 50% deposit of the total photography package price is required to secure a booking. The booking is only confirmed once the deposit has been received. The remaining 50% balance must be paid by the agreed date, preferably before or on the day of the photoshoot.

2. Cancellations and Refunds

Deposits and payments are subject to the cancellation policy agreed upon at the time of booking and applicable law. Clients must notify the photographer as early as possible if they wish to cancel. No-shows and last-minute cancellations may result in forfeiture of the deposit, subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the cancellation.

3. Rescheduling and Changes to Bookings

Clients must notify the photographer in advance of any requested changes to the booking date, time, location, or photography package. Changes made without reasonable prior notice may result in additional charges, loss of the original booking slot, or forfeiture of the deposit where permitted by law. Any rescheduling is subject to the photographer's availability.

4. Late Arrival

Clients are expected to arrive at the agreed time. Late arrival may reduce the available photoshoot time. Any extension will depend on the photographer's availability and may incur an additional fee.

5. Outstanding Payments

The outstanding balance must be paid according to the agreed payment schedule. The photographer reserves the right to postpone delivery of the final edited photographs until the outstanding balance has been settled, where permitted by law.

6. Delivery of Photographs

Edited photographs will be delivered within the estimated turnaround time communicated to the client. Delivery times may vary depending on workload, the selected package, and unforeseen circumstances.

7. Client Cooperation and Photography Locations

Clients are responsible for arriving prepared and obtaining any necessary permissions for locations they choose, unless otherwise agreed. The photographer will make reasonable efforts to deliver the agreed service.

8. Agreement to Terms

Clients must be able to review and accept these Terms and Conditions before completing a booking request. Submitting a booking requires agreeing to these terms via the designated checkbox on the booking form.
